01The problem

Sky surveys find more short-lived explosions than any telescope can follow. So future spacecraft will have to decide for themselves what to measure next.

Today's planners pick the measurement that best separates the object types they already know. That works, until something new shows up. A posterior over known classes always sums to one, so every extra measurement pushes the planner towards whichever known class fits least badly. It gets more sure exactly when it should get less sure.

We give the planner one more option, h₀, "none of the above", and we let it measure only forward in time, as a real spacecraft must.
